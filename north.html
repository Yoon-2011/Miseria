North Yangon

Protecting green spaces, reducing plastic waste, and creating cleaner communities for a healthier Yangon City.

What Problems Does North Yangon Face?

The North Yangon is more seriously wasting energy, increasingly consuming electricity, and suffers from increasing temperatures, and insufficient awareness of sustainable living. Many residents rely too heavily on appliances, leave the lights on for extended periods, and consume more energy than necessary

Smart & Sustainable Solutions

Realistic habits and affordable changes that can improve daily life while helping the environment in South Yangon.

Turn Off Unused Electronics

Turning off unused appliances avoids excessive use of electricity.

Use Natural Ventilation

Opening windows during cooler hours reduces excessive fan and air-conditioner usage.

Replace Old Bulbs With LED Lights

LED bulbs use less electricity and last longer than traditional bulbs.

Reduce Excessive Air-Conditioner Use

Using fans or natural airflow during cooler weather lowers energy waste.

Charge Devices Efficiently

Disconnecting chargers when not in use saves electricity

Use Digital Learning Materials

Digital notes and documents reduce paper waste and printing costs.

Reuse Household Items

Repairing and reusing items reduces unnecessary consumption and waste.

Encourage Energy Awareness

Teaching sustainable habits at home and schools increases long-term environmental awareness.

Use Natural Daylight

Opening curtains during daytime reduces electricity use from lighting.

Why Small Changes Matter

The environmental strain in North Yangon due to heavy electricity consumption and unsustainable energy consumption behaviors is increasing. Many residential homes and buildings are found with lights on for hours without need and having devices unnecessarily turned on for long periods of time as well as relying on cooling fans even if ventilation through natural air currents is an option. By habitually committing to these energy-wasting behaviors, this adds to energy use and is not environmentally friendly throughout the entire city.


These energy wasting habits are common. By practicing more energy efficient behaviors, large populations can contribute significantly to overall waste reduction through reduced energy consumption. If citizens have a habit of turning off appliances they are not using and have energy efficient lighting, while reducing unnecessary electricity use and utilizing natural ventilation can contribute greatly to saving energy and building more sustainable habits in the future.

Smart Swaps For Daily Life

Sustainable living doesn't have to be about pricey technology. Affordable little swaps can cut down waste and make the day-to-day in all parts of South Yangon better.

A Day In North Yangon

Realistic sustainable actions that citizens can practice throughout the day without major cost or lifestyle changes.

Morning

Use Natural Light And Airflow

Open windows and curtains during cooler hours instead of immediately relying on lights and air-conditioners.

Afternoon

Reduce Unnecessary Electricity Usage

Turn off unused electronics and unplug chargers to reduce energy waste during the day.

Evening

Create Energy-Efficient Habits

Use energy-saving lighting and reduce excessive appliance usage during nighttime hours.

Community Action Matters

Residents of North Yangon communities can inspire energy conservation through educational campaigns, school activities and shared sustainable habits. Collaboration can result in a decrease in energy waste, and cleanlier, environmentally responsible neighborhoods.
