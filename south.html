South Yangon

Protecting green spaces, reducing plastic waste, and creating cleaner communities for a healthier Yangon City.

What Problems Does South Yangon Face?

Environmental issues are also becoming a problem to South Yangon with the overuse of plastic items, large number of people using public spaces, littering, lack of green areas and so on. Waste management system in many communities are not efficient thus creating pollution and unclean environment.

All these problems affect nature and mankind. Plastic wastes are being found in the rivers and roads, and because less trees and green spaces are being developed in the area it makes the air quality poorer and the streets feel more hotter and unpleasant.

Smart & Sustainable Solutions

Realistic habits and affordable changes that can improve daily life while helping the environment in South Yangon.

Use Refillable Water Bottles

Buying bottled water daily creates unnecessary plastic waste. Refillable bottles are cheaper long-term and help reduce pollution.

Choose Reusable Shopping Bags

Cloth bags last longer than plastic bags and reduce waste that often ends up blocking drains and polluting streets.

Reduce Electricity During Daytime

Open windows and use natural sunlight instead of keeping lights and fans running unnecessarily during the day.

Separate Household Waste

Keeping recyclable waste separate from food waste makes recycling easier and helps reduce overflowing garbage areas.

Use Public Transportation More Often

Walking, cycling, or using public transportation for shorter trips helps reduce fuel pollution and traffic congestion.

Support Local Markets

Buying local products reduces transportation pollution while also supporting community businesses and local workers.

Plant Small Trees Or Indoor Plants

Plants improve air quality, cool surrounding areas, and create healthier living environments in crowded neighborhoods.

Avoid Food Waste

Only buying the amount of food needed helps reduce waste and saves money for households.

Use Digital Notes When Possible

Reducing unnecessary paper use helps conserve resources and decreases waste production.

Why Small Changes Matter

Many of the issues of environmental problem that occur in South Yangon have originated from the repeated daily routines that are accumulating over time. Huge plastic waste, littering and inappropriate refuse disposal tend to clog drains (particularly during rainy season) which causes flooding to be prevalent within streets and residential areas. Excessive use of space and waste causes unhygienic conditions and health problem for the communities.


Even one person does a little act does not seem like big deal but 1000's of city dwellers implementing positive habit on a daily basis, it can impact on the city to a certain great extend. Making a simple decision like reducing plastic, sorting your waste properly, keeping public place clean could prevent more flooding in a long run and enhance cleaner environment for the community.

Smart Swaps For Daily Life

Sustainable living doesn't have to be about pricey technology. Affordable little swaps can cut down waste and make the day-to-day in all parts of South Yangon better.

Instead Of
Try This Instead
Buying bottled drinks every day
Bring refillable bottles from home
Using plastic bags at markets
Carry reusable cloth bags

A Day In South Yangon

Realistic sustainable actions that citizens can practice throughout the day without major cost or lifestyle changes.

Morning

Prepare Before Leaving Home

Refill water bottles at home, bring reusable containers, and avoid buying unnecessary plastic-packaged items while outside.

Afternoon

Reduce Urban Pollution

Walk shorter routes when possible and support nearby local shops instead of increasing traffic and transportation pollution.

Evening

Keep Communities Cleaner

Separate recyclable waste, reduce electricity usage at night, and help prevent drains from becoming blocked with trash.

Community Action Matters

Sustainable change becomes stronger when communities work together. Simple actions like neighborhood cleanups, planting trees, and encouraging eco-friendly habits can improve South Yangon for everyone.

Young people especially play an important role in creating a cleaner future through awareness, education, and positive daily habits.
