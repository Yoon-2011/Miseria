Local Examples

Yangon's Path Toward a Smarter Future

Yangon is Myanmar’s largest city and faces many urban challenges such as traffic congestion, pollution, flooding, and inefficient public services. However, several local examples already show how the city is moving toward becoming smarter and more sustainable.

Through better transportation systems, renewable energy, green public spaces, and digital innovation, Yangon can improve quality of life while protecting the environment for future generations.

Current Local Examples

Yangon Circular Railway

The Yangon Circular Railway is one of the city’s most important public transportation systems. It connects many neighborhoods across Yangon and helps reduce road congestion by encouraging people to use trains instead of private vehicles.

Improving the railway with digital ticket systems, cleaner stations, and real-time train tracking can make it more efficient and attractive for commuters.

Kandawgyi & People's Park

Public parks provide valuable green spaces for recreation, biodiversity, and cleaner air. Parks help reduce the urban heat effect and create healthier environments for residents.

Smart irrigation systems, solar lighting, and eco-friendly waste bins can improve the sustainability of these green spaces.

Solar Street Lighting

Solar-powered street lights are already being introduced in some parts of Yangon. They reduce electricity costs while using clean renewable energy.

Expanding solar lighting across roads, neighborhoods, and parks can improve public safety while lowering carbon emissions.

Rainwater Harvesting

Yangon experiences heavy rainfall during the monsoon season, making rainwater harvesting an effective sustainable solution.

Collected rainwater can be used for watering plants, cleaning public spaces, and reducing pressure on the city’s water supply.

Smart Waste Management

Waste disposal and plastic pollution are major challenges in Yangon. Smart waste bins and improved recycling systems can make garbage collection more efficient.

Community recycling programs can also encourage residents to reduce waste and protect the environment.

Smart Traffic Management

Yangon’s traffic congestion causes delays, pollution, and wasted fuel. Smart traffic lights and AI-based traffic monitoring can improve traffic flow.

Better road coordination and public transport planning can reduce travel times and improve road safety.
