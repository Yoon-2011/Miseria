Smart Yangon City

Encouraging greener lifestyles, cleaner communities, and smarter sustainable habits across Yangon City. Small daily actions can create a healthier and more beautiful future.

Why Yangon Needs Sustainable Change

Many problems have led to various social and environmental concerns for people in the Yangon City everyday life. Pollution, plastic waste, floods, emission from traffic and high energy consumption keep the environment deteriorate and limit the quality of life.

This project is trying to provide more awareness towards people and encourage their behavior toward smarter decision and sustainable life. Each page of each district examines some localized problem and propose easy ways toward eco-friendlier habits that can be easily implemented in every resident life.

Yangon Districts

Explore sustainability goals across different parts of Yangon City.

East Yangon

Increased amounts of plastic refuse, dirty roads, blocked drainage systems and improper disposal behavior, in East Yangon can also be linked to health problems of local communities due to flooding, and other problems such as a lack of hygienic conditions.

West Yangon

West Yangon faces the challenges of water pollution and air pollution and traffic jams. The environmental degradation (water pollution, air pollution) and impacts on human well being are directly related with transport and waste.

South Yangon

In the southern region of Yangon, overconsumption of plastic, deficit in green public places and accumulating garbage in areas with high density populations result in lower urban aesthetics.

North Yangon

Energy waste, high electricity consumption and lack of public awareness on sustainable practices are prevailing issues in the North Yangon area. These issues lead to increased consumption of resources and higher environmental impacts.
