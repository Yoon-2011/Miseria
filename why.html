Smarter & Sustainable Future for Yangon

Yangon was chosen because it stands at the heart of Myanmar’s economic, cultural, and urban identity. As the country’s largest city and commercial center, Yangon has the greatest potential to lead national progress through smart and sustainable development. As a megacity with millions of inhabitants, expanding infrastructure and an increasing online presence, Yangon offers the perfect case study on the ways in which modern cities are capable of change without sacrificing their culture, communities and long-term viability.

Why Yangon?

Why Yangon Was Selected

Economic Importance

Yangon is responsible for a substantial portion of the business, trade, logistics, and investment taking place in Myanmar. The impact it has on the nation's economy is the most significant to the application of innovation and smart cities.

Urban Growth

Being one of the fastest growing cities in the country, Yangon still seems to be luring in more people, companies, and investment. The quick expansion of the city shows the growing need to plan for the future.

Cultural Significance

But Yangon is more than just a city of finance - it is one of history, architecture, ethnicity and identity. In its ability to reconcile the preservation of heritage with the push towards the future, Yangon stands out uniquely.

Strategic Regional Position

Positioned to take advantage of major sea trade routes and link to regional markets, Yangon is well placed to emerge as a leader among smart and sustainable cities in Southeast Asia.

Yangon's Future Significance

Yangon will set the direction for Myanmar's urban development and will therefore be a perfect testbed for sustainable infrastructure, intelligent technologies and resilient urban growth, since it will be influenced by both of the advantages and disadvantages brought by the country's accelerated urbanization.


Yangon, with its economic power, large population, significant position, and cultural importance, can provide the solid basis for continuous progress and a sustainable development future.
