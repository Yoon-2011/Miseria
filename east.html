East Yangon

Protecting green spaces, reducing plastic waste, and creating cleaner communities for a healthier Yangon City.

What Problems Does East Yangon Face?

Issues faced by the East of Yangon include accumulation of litter, filling up of garbage disposal sites, blocked drains and increasing plastic pollution. Both inadequate garbage management and lack of awareness regarding recycling activities affect the health of people and the environment. If a garbage disposal site gets blocked during the rainy season, flooding is more likely.

Smart & Sustainable Solutions

Realistic habits and affordable changes that can improve daily life while helping the environment in South Yangon.

Use Recycling Bins

Recycling materials correctly means less waste accumulation and the promotion of recycling habits.

Reduce Plastic Packaging

Choosing products with less packaging helps lower long-term waste production.

Bring Reusable Bottles

Reusable bottles reduce plastic pollution and are more affordable over time.

Dispose Waste Responsibly

Throwing trash properly keeps streets cleaner and protects drainage systems.

Save Water At Home

Turning off taps properly reduces unnecessary water waste.

Use Rechargeable Batteries

Rechargeable batteries produce fewer amounts of waste products than disposable batteries.

Support Community Cleanups

Community cleanups help the public spaces in a neighborhood and promote green practices.

Reduce Paper Waste

Using digital notes reduces unnecessary paper consumption.

Avoid Littering in Public Areas

Clean streets and sidewalks enhance the quality of the environment and the health of the community.

Why Small Changes Matter

The environment problems found in East Yangon generally come from the problem in waste disposal management and environmental awareness concerning recycling. There is a need to reduce many of people’s daily habit in discarding trash on the road, throwing mixed waste and a lot of single-use goods which will bring to garbage to be spread everywhere on public space. The accumulation of waste into dumping site and clogged drainages will eventually lead to flood during rainy season and poor sanitation environment to our neighbor.


Minor adjustments to our daily routines would lead to significant improvements if it could be done by a community. Simple steps such as segregation for recyclables, minimization of wasted packaging and appropriate waste disposal would directly result in more organized and clean environment while easing burden from waste disposal management system. Ultimately these practices instill environmental conscience in student population, households and residents, making the community a healthier environment for all to live.

A Day In East Yangon

Realistic sustainable actions that citizens can practice throughout the day without major cost or lifestyle changes.

Morning

Start With Less Waste

Bring reusable bottles and avoid buying unnecessary single-use plastic items before school or work.

Afternoon

Practice Cleaner Public Habits

Use proper waste bins in public spaces and encourage cleaner surroundings by avoiding littering.

Evening

Organize Household Waste

Separate recyclable materials and reduce unnecessary water and electricity waste at home.

Community Action Matters

Communities in East Yangon can improve public cleanliness through recycling drives, environmental education, and neighborhood cleanup activities. Schools and families working together can help build stronger awareness about sustainable living habits.
