About This Project

This project was created by Sangria Year 7 Group-3 students to explore how digital tools can help build a smarter and more sustainable future for Yangon. Through research, design, and innovative thinking, our goal is to create a user-friendly website that encourages citizens to adopt better daily habits that support both the environment and the community.

Our website is designed to guide users through simple everyday actions—such as reducing waste, saving water, choosing sustainable transport, and supporting social responsibility—that can lead to meaningful real-world behavior change. By making sustainability easier to understand and apply, we hope to inspire positive action across Yangon.

Project goals

The main goal of this project is to use technology to educate and empower people. We aim to make sustainability practical and achievable through digital guidance, interactive content, and local examples that connect directly to Yangon’s challenges.

Using Digital Tools for Change

Digital tools allow us to communicate ideas in interactive and engaging ways. Through website design, animations, educational resources, and practical action guides, we can help users understand sustainability and motivate them to take action.

Our platform is designed not just to inform, but to encourage habit-building. Users can explore solutions, track actions, and discover realistic ways to improve their environmental and social impact every day.

What Our Website Offers

Daily Sustainable Actions

Step-by-step suggestions that help users make smarter choices every day, from reducing plastic use to conserving water and electricity.

Local Yangon Solutions

Realistic and practical examples of sustainable innovations that can work in Yangon’s neighborhoods, schools, and public spaces.

Educational Resources

Easy-to-understand information about environmental issues, smart city ideas, and why sustainability matters.
