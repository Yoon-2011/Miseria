West Yangon

Protecting green spaces, reducing plastic waste, and creating cleaner communities for a healthier Yangon City.

What Problems Does West Yangon Face?

In West Yangon, traffic, air, water and plastic pollution exists, and is worsening. Congestion on city streets creates large amounts of exhaust emissions, and poor disposal of waste causes contamination of river and drainage networks, degrading the environment and public areas of inhabitants.

Smart & Sustainable Solutions

Realistic habits and affordable changes that can improve daily life while helping the environment in West Yangon.

Use Public Transportation

Traffic congestion is reduced and carbon emissions is lowered around the city by sharing transportation.

Reduce Single-Use Plastics

The use of alternatives to plastic straws, cups and take-away packaging will reduce long term pollution.

Reduce Electricity During Daytime

Open windows and use natural sunlight instead of keeping lights and fans running unnecessarily during the day.

Walk Short Distances

Walking nearby instead of driving reduces fuel usage and urban air pollution..

Dispose Of Waste Properly

Proper disposal of waste stops the pollution of rivers and streams, and prevents drain blockages.

Use Energy Efficient Appliances

Energy-saving devices lower our electricity bills and home energy costs.

Carry Reusable Containers

Reusable containers cut back on takeaway waste and plastic pollution.

Avoid Burning Waste

Garbage burning, for instance, emits hazardous substances into the air which take negative impacts on the health of public.

Encourage Green Public Areas

The introduction of trees and maintaining parks will also enhance air quality in heavily trafficked city districts.

Why Small Changes Matter

West Yangon's daily challenges include a build up of traffic and growing environmental pollution caused by increased transportation. Millions of cars on the roads discharge polluted air, which damages the overall environment, including increasing the town's temperatures. River and water pollution also rises as waste continues to be discharged into rivers and open spaces. Over a prolonged period, such practices can cause the surroundings to become increasingly damaged and the public spaces less hygienic and safe to use.


When many people perform these small sustainability acts regularly they can make a huge difference. If people choose to use public transport, walk short distances or reduce their usage of plastics, they can greatly contribute to helping their environment across the town. When everyone plays their part in trying to perform their duties wisely, a cleaner town, less polluted air and safe public spaces are all achievable.

Sustainable living doesn't have to be about pricey technology. Affordable little swaps can cut down waste and make the day-to-day in all parts of South Yangon better.

A Day In West Yangon

Realistic sustainable actions that citizens can practice throughout the day without major cost or lifestyle changes.

Morning

Travel Smarter

If you are able, please use public transport or shared vehicles. This helps cut down on traffic and vehicle emissions.

Afternoon

Reduce Pollution During Busy Hours

Walk shorter distances instead of using vehicles unnecessarily and support local businesses with sustainable practices.

Evening

Keep Waterways Cleaner

Discard your waste in an appropriate manner; don't throw garbage into the rivers, drains, roads, and street corners, once the bustle of the city is over.

Community Action Matters

Clean habits can be fostered in West Yangon neighborhoods through organized cleanup drives, recycling programs, and awareness activities. Public participation will contribute towards cleanliness of the city and minimize pollution and promote clean habitat.
